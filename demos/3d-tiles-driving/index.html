Monaco Drive

An arcade car on the real streets of Monte Carlo, streamed live from Google's Photorealistic 3D Tiles. The car raycasts against the photogrammetry mesh every frame to stay on the road — the famous Grand Prix corners are checkpoints.

Needs the same Map Tiles API key as the fly-through demo. W/S throttle & brake, A/D steer, C camera, R reset to start line.
